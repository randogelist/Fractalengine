FRAKTALFAKTUR 1.8.6 - QQRH Hilbert development

Finite source model, unchanged from 1.8.5:
  b_n = 200^n a_n/a_0, from theta_coefficients.hpp
  R in [0.1,1.25], r=200R, N=12
  v_n = b_n R^n / sqrt(sum_(j=0..N) b_j^2 R^(2j))
  w(z) = (v_n z^n)_(n=0..N).
There are 13 components. The table contains floating-point values, with no
rigorous full-function truncation enclosure.

HOLomorphic OVERLAP KERNEL
  H(z) = sum_(n=0..N) v_n^2 z^n = <w(z),v>.
Here the Hermitian inner product is linear in its first argument.
The full two-point Gram kernel is K(z,w)=H(z conjugate(w)).
  K(z,z) = ||w(z)||^2 > 0 for every finite z.
In particular H(1)=1. A zero of H(z) describes orthogonality to the reference
state v. It is not a claim about a zero of Theta, xi, or zeta.

PROJECTIVE DISTANCE
  coherence(z) = |H(z)|^2 / K(z,z)
  d_FS([w(z)],[v]) = acos(sqrt(coherence(z))).
The distance is in radians, in [0,pi/2], with zero at z=1.
This is the distance in ambient complex projective space, not the geodesic
distance constrained to the image of the curve z -> [w(z)].

PROJECTIVE METRIC
With convention ds^2=g(z)|dz|^2 (no additional factor of 4),
  g(z) = d_z d_conjugate(z) log K(z,z)
       = (||w||^2 ||w'||^2 - |<w',w>|^2)/||w||^4.
Writing a_n=v_n^2 and t=|z|^2 gives the nonnegative expression
  g(z) = sum_(i<j) (j-i)^2 a_i a_j t^(i+j-1)
         / (sum_n a_n t^n)^2.
Consequently g(0)=a_1/a_0. Away from zero it is also Var_p(n)/|z|^2,
where p_n=a_n |z|^(2n)/sum_j a_j |z|^(2j).
The metric is radial: a metric-only field has rotational symmetry. It measures
local distinguishability of neighboring coherent states, not a fractal orbit.
The implemented pair sum avoids cancellation in the Gram determinant. Outside
the unit circle it uses reversed coefficients and normalized weights; extreme
coordinates may underflow the very small metric to zero.

NEW DYNAMICAL CONSTRUCTIONS
Kernel Newton iterates z_next=z-H(z)/H'(z), from a pixel seed z.
Kernel feedback iterates z_next=z^2+c-0.35 H(z)/H'(z).
These newly defined finite rational maps use the Hilbert overlap polynomial.
The feedback parameter portrait uses z_0=0; a Julia portrait varies the seed
for a fixed c. Small H' is treated as a numerical pole. Any display bailout
is a rendering cutoff, not a certified escape radius. Newton convergence
requires a small correction as well as a small residual. Colours encode
numeric orbit information, not formally certified distinct roots.

SCOPE
The old overlap, Hardy-shift and angular-kernel views are retained. These
finite Hilbert identities and numerical dynamics do not supply the missing
full-Theta tail bounds, prove positivity of the research shell kernel, or
establish RH. No Lean compilation or theorem certification is performed.
